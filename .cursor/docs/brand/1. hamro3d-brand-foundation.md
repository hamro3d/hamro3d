# 01. Brand Foundation

## Ultimate Goal

Build a premium, emotionally-driven craft brand that preserves human moments in physical form.

We do not simply create products.  
We create **objects of memory, identity, and emotional connection** — designed to be kept, displayed, and felt.

**Timeline:** 5-year journey from craft studio to premium figurine brand  
**Team:** 2–3 founding members, growing deliberately  

---

## Core Purpose

**To preserve meaningful human moments in tangible form.**

---

## Core Promise

**We turn meaningful human moments into objects you can keep forever.**

---

## Taglines

**Primary (Long-Term):**  
*Where emotions take tangible form.*

**Phase 0:**  
*Crafted with care. Made to mean something.*

**Phase 1:**  
*Where your story takes shape.*

## Core Identity

**Hamro3D is a memory-to-object studio.**

Not:
- A 3D printing service  
- A gift shop  
- A product-based business  

Technology is a tool.  
Emotion is the product.

# 02. Phase Roadmap

---

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Now → Year 1)

### Reality
- Low capital  
- Entry-level technology  
- 2–3 person team  

### Products
- PLA-based creations:
  - Keychains  
  - Lithophanes / Litholamps  
  - Moon lamps  
  - Nameplates / Name signs  
  - Object holders  
  - Puzzles  
  - Early stylized figurines  

### Goal
- Build craft discipline  
- Establish brand habits  
- Acquire early customers  
- Generate financial runway  

### Brand Message
*Thoughtful handcrafted pieces, made with care.*

### Strategic Focus
- Every product must feel **personal and gift-worthy**
- Emphasize **story and meaning**, not function
- Build perception of **care and intention**, not “premium” claims

### Do NOT
- Overpromise realism  
- Position as a “3D printing service”  
- Release generic, emotionless products  

---

## Phase 1 — Growth (Year 1–3)

### Reality
- Improved tools and materials  
- Expanded small team  
- Growing portfolio  

### Products
- Early custom figurines:
  - Busts  
  - Simplified human figures  
- Continued personalized product line  

### Goal
- Establish figurine capability  
- Build reputation for personalization  
- Strengthen emotional positioning  

### Brand Message
*Where your story takes shape.*

---

## Phase 2 — Premium (Year 3–5)

### Reality
- Refined technology  
- Established brand presence  
- Recognized premium positioning  

### Products
- Highly realistic, emotionally-driven human figurines  
- Memory-based custom pieces  

### Goal
- Become the premium destination for personalized figurines in Nepal and beyond  

### Brand Message
*Where emotions take tangible form.*

---

# 03. Strategic Filter (CRITICAL)

Every decision must pass this test:

> **“Does this feel like a memory — or just a product?”**

If it feels like just a product → it does not align with the brand.

---

# 04. Brand Discipline Rules

- Do not sell features — sell meaning  
- Do not highlight tools — highlight outcomes  
- Do not chase volume — build significance  
- Do not rush — refine  

---

# 05. Long-Term Vision

Hamro3D becomes:

- A brand people trust with **their most meaningful moments**
- A studio known for **emotional realism**
- A category-defining name in **memory-based physical art**

---

# Final Note

We are not building a product company.

We are building a **brand that turns human emotion into something you can hold.**
