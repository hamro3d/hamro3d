OUR STORY · हाम्रो कथा

We turn emotions into objects you can hold.

Hamro3D exists for one reason: to give weight to what matters. We are a Kathmandu studio building premium keepsakes — figurines, lamps, and engraved pieces — so love, remembrance, and celebration never stay only on a screen.

WHERE WE BEGAN

Born in the valley, shaped like craft

A small studio with a large heart

We founded Hamro3D in Kathmandu in 2025 because Nepal deserves gifting that feels international in quality yet intimate in meaning. 3D printing here is not a novelty — it is a discipline we treat like woodworking or metalwork: patient, iterative, and guided by the person the piece is for.

Each commission starts with your story — a wedding in Patan, a parent remembered after Tihar, a friendship that survived distance. We translate that narrative into material choices, scale, finish, and packaging worthy of Bagmati roads and living-room shelves alike.

Pricing is honest in NPR. Timing is clear. The result is something they keep on a desk, near a bed, or beside a lamp — not another object forgotten in a drawer.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Principles we will not trade away

Emotional Authenticity over Technical Output

A flawless print that feels cold is a failure. We chase the moment the recipient recognises themselves — or someone they love — in the object.

Meaning over Utility

Our pieces are meant to be kept, not consumed. If it only “does a job,” it can wait; if it carries memory, it belongs on Hamro3D.

Craft over Speed

Same-day churn is not our model. We sand, cure, finish, and inspect so what leaves Thamel-bound courier bags feels studio-made, not factory-stamped.

Quality over Price

We price fairly in NPR for premium materials and time. We would rather decline a rushed discount than deliver something we would not give our own family.

HOW WE CRAFT

From your words to their hands

  1. 01

    You share the memory

    Photos, dates, nicknames, inside jokes — whatever anchors the story. We read every detail you offer from here in Kathmandu before a nozzle ever moves.

  2. 02

    We shape it by hand

    Digital modelling, print strategy, post-processing, and finish are all touched by our team. Machines assist; humans decide.

  3. 03

    You hold it forever

    Gift-ready packaging travels across the Kathmandu Valley and beyond, so the unboxing is the first chapter they remember.
